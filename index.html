Support

Pixel Album is a book of nonogram puzzles. Each grid hides a small pixel picture. Paint the cells the numbers ask for, and when the grid is done the picture is pasted into its album. If something isn't working, or you have a question, write to us.

How do I find my way around?

The album has four ribbon bookmarks: Today, Albums, Endless and Stats. Tap a ribbon to turn to that page. Settings are behind the gear on the Today page.

How do I play?

  • The numbers next to each row and above each column are the lengths of the painted blocks in that line, in order. "3 1" means a block of 3, at least one empty cell, then a block of 1.
  • Tap a cell to paint it. Drag to paint a straight line.
  • Switch to the cross tool to mark cells you know are empty. You don't have to mark them all to finish.
  • On big grids, pinch to zoom and move around with two fingers. The numbers stay at the edges of the screen.
  • Numbers turn gray when their block is done.

Do I ever have to guess?

No. Every puzzle in the app has exactly one solution, and you can reach it by looking at one row or column at a time. We check every picture with a solver that uses only that kind of reasoning before it goes into the app.

How do hints work?

A hint finds a row or column where you can make a sure step, highlights it and explains why, for example "The 8-block in a 10-cell row must cover the middle 6 cells." Tap Show Me to fill those cells. If one of your marks is wrong, the hint points to it first. Without any purchase you get 3 hints a day. If you own any album you get 10 a day, and with All Albums hints are unlimited. The count starts over each day. Best times count only for puzzles solved without hints.

What are Show mistakes and 3 lives?

Show mistakes tints any row or column that has a wrong mark. 3 lives is an optional challenge: a wrong cell is caught right away and costs a life. Turn either on in Settings, under Gameplay. They apply to puzzles you start from then on.

What is the Daily Picture?

A new picture every day, open to everyone. Solve it on the day to grow your streak. The next one appears at midnight in your time zone. The calendar of Daily Pictures runs through September 2027. You can turn on one quiet reminder a day in Settings; it only comes if you haven't solved that day's picture yet.

What is Endless?

Puzzles made on your phone for when you just want to play. They end in an abstract pattern instead of a picture. Without any purchase you can play five 10×10 puzzles a day. Owning any album adds 15×15 and 20×20 and raises the limit to 15 a day. All Albums makes Endless unlimited.

How do I add the widgets?

Home Screen: touch and hold an empty spot, tap Edit, then Add Widget, search for Pixel Album and choose Daily Picture or Latest Picture.

Lock Screen: touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, then Lock Screen, tap the widget area and choose Pixel Album to see your streak.

What can I buy?

The Starter album and the Daily Picture come with the app. Every other album sits on the shelf sealed until it's yours: open its page to see what's inside, then buy it. Each album is a one-time purchase, and All Albums is a single one-time purchase that opens every album, including ones we add later. Purchases work with Family Sharing. There are no ads and no subscription.

How do I restore purchases?

Tap the gear on the Today page, then tap Restore Purchases under Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the Apple Account you used to buy them.

Which languages does the app speak?

English and 28 other languages: Ukrainian, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil and Portugal), Japanese, Korean, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Polish, Turkish, Dutch, and every other language of the European Union except Irish and Maltese. It follows your iPhone's language.

Will my progress move to a new iPhone?

Progress is stored on your iPhone and comes along when you restore a device backup.
