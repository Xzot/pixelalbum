Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

Pixel Album ("the app") is developed by Vladyslav Shchuka ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

What we collect

Nothing. We don't run servers, the app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it contains no third-party SDKs.

Data stored on your device

Your finished pictures, puzzles in progress, statistics, streak and settings are stored only on your iPhone. Your streak, today's Daily Picture and your latest picture are kept in storage shared with the app's widgets so they can show them. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Daily reminder

The reminder is off unless you turn it on. It's a notification your iPhone schedules for itself at the time you choose; nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. You can turn it off in the app's Settings or in iOS Settings.

Purchases

Albums are sold by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app only asks Apple which albums your Apple Account owns.

Game Center

Game Center is optional. If you're signed in, the app sends your Daily Picture times and the number of pictures you've collected to its leaderboards and reports achievements. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; we don't receive this data.

Sharing

When you share a picture, the app creates an image and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes is up to you; the app doesn't send it anywhere by itself.

Children

The app is rated 4+. We don't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
